FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You edit everything from the sidebar. If you do know code, the exported HTML is simple to customize further.

Where can I publish my website?

Anywhere that hosts static files: GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, or your own hosting. Upload the exported HTML file and you're live.

Is my work saved?

Yes. The builder auto-saves in your browser. Export your site to keep a permanent copy.

Does it work on mobile?

The sites you build are fully responsive, and you can preview the phone layout while you edit.
